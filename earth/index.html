our mission

Every camera in the world is a sensor. Most of them are wasted because no one is watching. Gods Eye puts an AI agent behind every feed — so the world's eyes are finally open.

HOW IT WORKS

A self-driving security room.

Three steps. No humans in the loop. You only see the dispatches that matter.

01

Camera streams in

Plug in your CCTV feeds or use our 8-city synthetic demo. Frames flow in over a secure SSE channel, no storage required.

02

AI agent watches

One Gemini agent per camera, booted in its own Linux sandbox. Runs skills like weapon detection, loitering, forced entry — reasons in real time.

03

You get the dispatch

Red, yellow, or green pin on a live 3D globe. Open it for the clip, the reasoning trace, and a one-tap dispatch action.
